隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 13 日 · 網站：https://3dcards.app.dreamsworld.top · 也適用於 3D Cards Windows 和 Android App

3D Cards 是一個開源的鐳射卡片製作工具。我們盡量讓你的資料留在你自己的裝置上。以下說明哪些資料會離開你的裝置、存在哪裡、存多久。

你的照片和卡片

  • 上傳照片、製作卡片、自動摳圖，全部在你的瀏覽器或 App 裡處理，照片不會上傳到我們的伺服器。
  • 做好的卡片存在你的瀏覽器（IndexedDB）或 App 的資料裡。清除瀏覽器網站資料或解除安裝 App 時，卡片會一起刪除。

分享連結

  • 只有在你按下「建立分享連結」時，那一張卡片（含照片）才會上傳到網站伺服器（Cloudflare），任何拿到連結的人都能看到。
  • 分享的卡片會在 14 天後自動刪除；你也可以隨時按「停止分享」立即刪除。

Google 登入與雲端同步

  • 使用「用 Google 同步」時，我們會向 Google 要求兩項權限：
    • drive.appdata：在你的 Google 雲端硬碟裡建立一個只有 3D Cards 能存取的隱藏資料夾，用來存放你的卡片。我們無法看到或存取你雲端硬碟裡的其他任何檔案。
    • email、openid：讀取你的 Google 帳號 ID 和電子郵件，用來在畫面上顯示「已登入：你的信箱」，以及在公開展廳辨認你的帳號（見下方）。
  • 你的卡片直接在你的裝置和你的 Google 雲端硬碟之間傳送，不會經過或存放在我們的伺服器。
  • 登入憑證（存取權杖）只保存在你的裝置上。在 App 裡登入時，憑證會在我們的伺服器暫存最多 10 分鐘以便交給 App，取走後立即刪除。
  • 按「登出」會撤銷授權並刪除裝置上的憑證。你也可以隨時到 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權 移除 3D Cards。
  • 3D Cards 對 Google API 所取得資訊的使用與傳輸，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」規定。我們不會出售、轉讓這些資料，也不會用於廣告或訓練人工智慧模型。

公開展廳、讚與倒讚

  • 登入 Google 後，你可以把自己的卡片送到公開展廳。送出時，卡片（含照片）會上傳到網站伺服器（Cloudflare），由網站管理員審核，通過後所有人都看得到。
  • 公開展廳會顯示你設定的公開名稱、卡片內容、讚和倒讚的數量、公開日期，不會顯示你的 Google 帳號或電子郵件。
  • 伺服器會保存：你的 Google 帳號 ID、電子郵件（只有管理員在審核時看得到，用來聯絡）、公開名稱、你送出的卡片，以及你按的讚和倒讚。
  • 你可以隨時下架自己公開的卡片，卡片和它的讚、倒讚會從伺服器刪除。要刪除整個帳號資料，請寄信到 axianggaming1211@gmail.com。

隱形水印

  • 建立分享連結或送到公開展廳時，照片會加上肉眼看不見的水印，記錄上傳者的公開名稱和一組代號（沒有登入時記錄為「unknown user」），用來處理版權爭議。
  • 水印不包含你的電子郵件或 Google 帳號。照片已經有別人的水印時會保留原本的水印。

第三方服務

  • Cloudflare：提供網站主機與分享連結的儲存，會依其政策處理一般連線記錄（例如 IP 位址）。
  • Google Fonts：載入網站字型。
  • jsDelivr：第一次使用「自動摳圖」時下載執行工具。
  • GitHub：App 檢查新版本、提供安裝檔下載。Google Play：從 Play 商店安裝時，由 Google 提供下載與更新。

我們沒有使用任何分析、追蹤或廣告工具，也沒有 Cookie。

兒童

本服務不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

變更與聯絡

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · https://3dcards.app.dreamsworld.top · Also applies to the 3D Cards Windows and Android apps

3D Cards is an open-source tool for making holographic cards. We keep your data on your own device wherever possible.

Your photos and cards

Photos, cards and automatic cut-outs are processed in your browser or the app (Windows, Android). Photos are not uploaded to our server. Cards are saved in your browser (IndexedDB) or in the app's data.

Share links

Only when you press “Create share link” is that one card (with its photos) uploaded to the site's server (Cloudflare), where anyone with the link can see it. Shared cards are deleted automatically after 14 days, or immediately when you press “Stop sharing”.

Google sign-in and cloud sync

“Sync with Google” requests drive.appdata, which lets 3D Cards create a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only 3D Cards can access to store your cards; it cannot see any other files in your Drive. It also requests email and openid, used to show which account you are signed in with and to recognise your account in the public gallery (see below). Cards travel directly between your device and your Google Drive and are not stored on our server. Sign-in tokens are kept on your device; during sign-in in the app they are held on our server for at most 10 minutes until the app collects them, then deleted. “Sign out” revokes access; you can also remove 3D Cards at Google Account › Third-party access.

3D Cards' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell or transfer this data, and do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Public gallery, likes and dislikes

Signed in with Google, you can send your own cards to the public gallery. The card (with its photos) is uploaded to the site's server (Cloudflare) and reviewed by the site owner; once approved, everyone can see it. The gallery shows the public name you choose, the card, its likes and dislikes and the date, never your Google account or e-mail. The server keeps your Google account ID, e-mail (seen only by the admin during review, for contact), public name, the cards you send and your likes and dislikes. You can take your public cards down at any time, which deletes them and their votes. To delete all your account data, e-mail axianggaming1211@gmail.com.

Invisible watermark

When you create a share link or send a card to the public gallery, the photo gets an invisible watermark with the uploader's public name and a short code (“unknown user” when not signed in), used to handle copyright disputes. It contains no e-mail or Google account. A photo that already carries someone else's watermark keeps it.

Third parties

Cloudflare (hosting and share links), Google Fonts (fonts), jsDelivr (cut-out tool, on first use) GitHub (app updates and downloads) and Google Play (when installed from the Play Store). We use no analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13.
